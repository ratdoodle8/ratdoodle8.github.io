Ratdoodle Adventure

Free Demo Available on Steam Now!

Explore an adorable Claymation inspired world as Doodle the Rat in this whimsical 3D Platforming Adventure!

After crash landing on an alien planet, Doodle finds that its inhabitants may need even more help than he does! A flood of strange machines has recently begun to take hold of the local wildlife, causing the otherwise docile creatures to attack anything and everything around them!

Use Doodle's Glitter Ability to float in the air, or launch around the stage with a targeted boost mechanic. Perform good deeds like saving friendly critters, restoring ancient temples, and freeing wildlife from the mind-controlling devices called Erasers to collect Glitter Sparks.

About Ratdoodle's World

Ratdoodle's World is a project exploring a world full of tiny spunky creatures I, Ratdoodle, found within the treacherous depths of the darkest corners of my evil, twisted mind.

I will be adding to this site as time goes on in an attempt to document my ideas and hopefully build cool experiences for weird messed up freaks like me.
