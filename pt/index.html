How your beet buddies work

There are three sections with six timed steps each. Start at the first step, focus on just that task, and let the timer move you along. When a step ends, its beet wakes up, celebrates, and the next step starts automatically. The little progress bar is your visual trail from “still sleepy” to “we did it!”

① Get ready · 17½ minutesGive the first task a full 5 minutes. The next five get 2½ minutes each.
② Make it · 9 minutesSix steps, with 1½ minutes for each one. Keep the momentum and make the video.
③ Post it · 9 minutesAnother six 1½-minute steps to finish the posting process. The whole session is 35½ minutes.

GO starts the session or resumes a pause. PAUSE freezes both clocks. RESET THIS TIMER restarts only the step you’re on, while RESET SESSION puts all 18 steps back to the beginning. Starting a fresh session plays its own sound; each finished step has a small celebratory jingle; finishing all 18 has a bigger finale.

Sound starts after you press GO, because browsers need a tap before they can play audio. You can leave the tab open while you work; if you close or refresh it, the timer starts over.
